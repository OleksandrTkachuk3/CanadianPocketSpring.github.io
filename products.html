Pocket springs

Pocket spring mattresses can have multiple support zones. Typically, these mattresses are divided into 3, 5, or 7 zones, each specifically designed to support different parts of the body such as the head, shoulders, back, and legs. These zones help the mattress conform better to the natural curves of the body.

  • Pocket spring diameter
  • Pocked spring height
  • Coil count per m2
  • Pocket spring wire diameter
  • Pocket spring unit length
  • Pocket spring unit width
  • Zone type (any number or row of zones is available)

Canadian Pocket Springs supplies Large and Small Canadian mattress manufacturers with standard and custom Pocket Spring/Coil Assemblies. Our Pocket Spring assemblies are found in some of the most premium Canadian mattress brands. We proudly manufacture Pocket Springs right here in Canada, with some of the most advanced manufacturing equipment, with exacting Quality Standards. We process both large orders and small specialty orders within short lead times. We have several constructions in our standard catalogue with the following feels: Plush, Medium, Medium-Firm, Firm. We can also customize coil configurations and zoning to suit your specific product and marketing needs. Contact us to find out how we can supply your pocket coil requirements.
